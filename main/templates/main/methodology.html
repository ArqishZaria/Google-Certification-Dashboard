Methodology - how every number is calculated

Data sources and how they are joined

  1. Registration master (Google Form export) - the profile of every applicant.
  2. Final list - the shortlisted candidates (screening). Linked to registration by CNIC, falling back to email. (Its "id" column is a serial number in most sheets, so it is never used as a key.)
  3. Coursera (two enterprise accounts): Program Activity = licences (MEMBER = accepted, INVITED = pending); Specialisation Activity and Course Activity = learning progress.

A Coursera licence is attached to a person in this order: manual override → email → CNIC (Coursera "External ID") → registration CNIC/email. Names are never used to merge people; look-alikes are only suggested for review. Anything ambiguous appears on the Data quality page.

Business rules (from the BRD)

  • One participant = one licence. A person with two licences is counted once (flagged).
  • Completed = at least one Professional Certificate marked Completed by Coursera. AI Essentials, Prompting Essentials, Agile Essentials and other short courses never increase the completion count; they are reported separately.
  • Progress % is measured against the Professional Certificate only: completed courses ÷ courses in the certificate (100% once completed). With several certificates, the most advanced one is used. If two versions of the same specialization exist (e.g. 7- and 8-course editions) they are merged into one.
  • Started = enrolled in at least one specialization or course.
  • Active learner = started, not completed, activity within {{ inactivity }} days of the data snapshot date. Inactive learner = started, not completed, no activity for longer. Not started = licence accepted, nothing enrolled. Therefore accepted = completed + active + inactive + not started.
  • Completion rate = completed ÷ accepted licences. Utilisation = accepted ÷ issued. Activation = started ÷ issued.
  • Monthly and weekly growth tables bucket dates in Pakistan time (UTC+5).
  • Inactivity is measured against the snapshot date of the Coursera export, not today's date, so numbers do not drift between imports.
  • Region / Local council / Area = where the person lives, from the registration form ("Residing Region", "Current Local Council", "JK Attendance") - the same as the 2025 Excel and BRD s.5. National Council staff (Coursera programme "National Employee") are therefore counted in the region where they live; use the Licence programme filter to isolate them. Without a registration record the Coursera programme region is used. Local-council and Jamatkhana names repeat across regions, so they are always shown with their region / council.
  • In progress = started and not completed (= active + inactive).

How the numbers relate to the 2025 Excel dashboard

The engine was verified against the workbook: feeding the workbook's own data through the same code reproduces all 18 national cards and all 99 per-region cards (python manage.py verify_excel_parity "<workbook>"). The dashboard's main figures follow the BRD, which deliberately differs in these ways; the Excel-compatible view on the dashboard shows the old definitions side by side.
